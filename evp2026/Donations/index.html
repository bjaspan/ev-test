Donations

Quite a few people have said they like the site so much they would like to make a contribution. If you would like to make a one-time contribution, you can do that using PayPal. We will use the contributions to promote and improve the site in various ways. There are costs for running the site (URL registration, the server, and more). We also use the money to hire people to help out and also to improve the site in various ways, and other things, including ads to promote it, etc.

We see this Website as a community service. Answering readers' questions on Saturdays and letting readers post their own thoughts on Sundays are examples of that.

A one-time donation can be made by clicking on the PayPal icon below. If you have an account at PayPal you can use it. If not, PayPal takes all major credit cards. You do not have to open a PayPal account first. If you don't know what PayPal is, click here. PayPal was designed to allow you to make payments to individuals and small businesses in a secure way without giving the recipient your credit card number. PayPal has over 350 million current accounts and executes millions of secure transactions per day. Your browser must accept cookies from PayPal to use it.
